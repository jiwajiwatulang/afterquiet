archival maintenance guide

care & quality.

our accessories and canvas objects are designed to be lived with, worn regularly, and incorporated into your everyday routine. here is how to maintain their longevity and tactile character.

enamel pins & die-struck badges

hard enamel // antique plating

cleaning & maintenance

wipe the surface gently with a soft dry microfiber cloth. avoid harsh chemical jewelry dips, abrasive sponges, or prolonged water exposure. if needed, lightly moisten the cloth with lukewarm water and dry immediately.

rubber clutches & attachment

our pins are equipped with dual heavy-duty rubber clutches for a secure grip on heavyweight canvas. press down firmly until seated. for high-friction placements on jackets or bags, inspect clutches periodically.

heavyweight 16oz canvas tote bags

raw organic cotton // unbleached

washing recommendations

spot clean minor marks with a damp cloth and mild soap. for a complete wash, gently cold hand wash inside-out. do not machine tumble dry or use chlorine bleach; doing so damages the natural organic fibers.

drying & pin styling

reshape the handles and hang dry naturally in a shaded, well-ventilated area. when styling pins from our Customizer Studio, push the pin posts between fabric weaves to avoid unnecessary puncturing.

screenprinted vinyl stickers

uv-cured ink // outdoor matte

surface preparation

thoroughly clean and degrease the target surface (laptops, water bottles, skateboards, notebooks) with rubbing alcohol and ensure it is 100% dry before peeling and sticking.

outdoor & wash durability

screenprinted with UV-cured outdoor ink on thick matte vinyl. weatherproof, waterproof, and dishwasher-friendly when applied to clean, smooth, non-porous surfaces.

need advice on care or replacements?

our team is always available to answer product care questions or assist with clutches.
