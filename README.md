# afterquiet

> *wear what you can't always say.*

**afterquiet** is an independent accessories brand exploring identity, emotions, personal expression, and visual culture through wearable objects and thoughtful storytelling.

---

## Brand Philosophy

an object can be more than an accessory. it can be a reminder, a personal statement, a memory, or a quiet form of resistance.

afterquiet embraces imperfection, individuality, emotional honesty, and the freedom to express yourself without needing everyone's approval.

### Core Principles
- **individuality**: expression should feel personal, never dictated by temporary algorithms.
- **emotional honesty**: contradictions, vulnerability, and restless thoughts are all part of being human.
- **independence**: maintaining a distinctive perspective instead of following popular mass trends.
- **creative resistance**: questioning social expectations and communicating quiet truths through graphic objects.
- **accessibility**: expressive pieces that people can easily incorporate into their everyday style (standard flat 5k series).

## Digital Storefront & Studio Features

### 1. Curated Catalogue (Flat 5K Standard)
- All curated pins, badges, stickers, and canvas carriers are standardized at **Rp 5.000 (flat 5k)**.
- Filterable by product category (*pins & badges, totes, bags*) as well as by release volume (*volume 01, volume 02*).

### 2. Interactive Product View
- High-contrast visual archive preview with edition stamps.
- Detailed specifications: material, finish, dimensions, backing, and concept narrative.
- Instant quantity selector and direct add-to-bag capability.

### 3. Custom Bag Studio (`/try-on`)
- Interactive digital canvas allowing visitors to select a base tote or tactical bag, then freely drag and position pins and badges onto the bag surface.
- Real-time calculation and one-click WhatsApp order generation with the exact custom setup.

### 4. Slide-Over Shopping Bag
- Seamless drawer interface calculating subtotals with direct WhatsApp checkout message formatting.

## Store Administration (`/admin`)

Shopify-like back-office management protected by a 4-digit administrator PIN (default: `1234`):

- **Dashboard Overview**: Key business indicators including total products, active listings, flat price benchmark, and estimated stock value.
- **Catalogue & Product Editor**: Comprehensive editor for titles, volumes, categories, prices, stock, tags, materials, and card graphics.
- **CSV Data Exchange**:
  - **Export CSV**: Download complete catalogue spreadsheet compatible with Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers.
  - **Import CSV**: Upload updated CSV files to bulk-update products, prices, and stock instantly.
- **Theme & Copy Customization**: Live control over announcement bar messages, hero headlines, brand philosophy, WhatsApp order routing number, and accent colors.
